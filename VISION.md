# Vision

## One-sentence vision

MissionPilot turns fragmented opportunity hunting into a transparent, continuously improving decision system that helps professionals find and win the right work without surrendering control to automation.

## The problem

Senior professionals waste substantial time across disconnected job boards, recruiter messages, company career pages and freelance platforms. Search results are noisy, filters are shallow, compensation is often hidden, “remote” frequently means geographically restricted or hybrid, and generic AI tools produce applications without enough evidence or traceability.

The result is a poor loop:

1. Search manually.
2. Open many low-quality listings.
3. Guess whether the role is truly compatible.
4. Rewrite the same application material repeatedly.
5. Receive little feedback.
6. Learn almost nothing from the outcome.

## The desired loop

MissionPilot creates a measurable feedback loop:

1. **Discover** opportunities from authorized sources.
2. **Normalize** them into a consistent structure.
3. **Filter** hard constraints before spending AI tokens.
4. **Evaluate** strategic, commercial and experiential fit.
5. **Explain** the score using evidence and uncertainty.
6. **Prepare** application material grounded in verified facts.
7. **Approve** every external action through a human gate.
8. **Observe** outcomes and explicit feedback.
9. **Improve** ranking, prompts and recommendations.
10. **Re-evaluate** previously seen opportunities when the profile changes.

## Long-term vision

The job and mission search domain is the first vertical. The underlying platform should later support other high-value knowledge workflows through reusable components:

- source adapters;
- structured extraction;
- policy and constraint engines;
- multi-step agent workflows;
- evaluation and critique loops;
- human approval gates;
- auditability;
- outcome learning.

MissionPilot is not intended to become an unsupervised “apply everywhere” bot. Its differentiation is judgment quality, evidence, control and continuous improvement.

## Product principles

### Human authority

The system may recommend and prepare. The user remains responsible for decisions and external actions.

### Evidence before eloquence

A persuasive output that is not grounded in verified experience is a defect.

### Hard constraints before soft scoring

A role that violates a mandatory constraint must not receive a deceptively high global score.

### Explainable uncertainty

Unknown compensation, ambiguous remote policy and missing contract details must be shown as uncertainty, not silently guessed.

### Source respect

The product uses public, licensed, user-provided or explicitly authorized sources. It does not circumvent technical protections.

### Modular intelligence

Providers, models, prompts, scoring rules and source adapters must be replaceable.

### Observable loops

Every workflow produces traceable runs, evaluations and failure states.

### Progressive autonomy

Autonomy increases only after demonstrated reliability and always remains reversible.

## Success definition

MissionPilot succeeds when it reduces time spent on irrelevant listings, increases the proportion of high-quality applications, improves interview conversion and helps the user understand why the system made each recommendation.
